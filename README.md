# LibreNotes

## What is LibreNotes?

LibreNotes is a free, open web platform where students can create an account and share their own study notes with others. Notes can be typed directly into the platform or uploaded as files (PDF or images).

Other users can browse, search, and filter notes by subject to find study materials faster instead of recreating notes from scratch.

### Core Problem

Students waste time recreating notes that already exist somewhere, and there is no easy, free, student-run platform dedicated to finding and sharing quality study materials.

## Key Policies

- **Free Forever** — No ads and no premium tiers.
- **Open to Anyone Online** — Anyone can access the platform, but an account is required to use its core features.
- **Own Notes Only** — Users may only upload their own work. Any referenced books or sources must be cited.
- **Community-Driven Moderation** — Users can flag inappropriate or low-quality notes. Once a note reaches the flag threshold, it is automatically hidden and sent to an admin review queue instead of requiring manual review of every upload.
- **Mobile-Friendly** — Built with Bootstrap for a responsive experience across devices.

## Stakeholders (Who Uses It?)

### 1. Student Contributors
Upload and share their own study notes with the community.

### 2. Student Browsers
Search, browse, view, and use study notes shared by others.

### 3. Admins / Moderators
Review flagged content, enforce community guidelines, and manage user accounts.

### 4. Guest Visitors
Preview the platform and its available content before signing up.

### 5. Original Authors / Copyright Holders
Indirect stakeholders whose work is protected by the platform's **Own Notes Only** policy and source citation requirements.

---

## Project Principles

1. **Accessibility** — Study materials should be easy to find and access.
2. **Community** — Students help one another learn by sharing knowledge.
3. **Integrity** — Contributors must upload their own work and cite external sources.
4. **Safety** — Community reporting and moderation help maintain a reliable collection of notes.
5. **Sustainability** — The platform remains free, without ads or premium tiers.
6. **Responsiveness** — The platform should work smoothly on mobile phones, tablets, and desktop computers.
