How it works

1. Browse

Search or filter notes by subject and education level. Guests can preview what is available.

2. Create an account

Sign up with your email and password to unlock uploads, upvotes, comments, and reports.

3. Share and help

Upload your own notes and upvote the ones that helped you study.

Community guidelines

A shared library only works if people can trust it.

Own notes only

Upload only your own work. If you reference a book or other source, cite it.

Community moderation

Anyone with an account can flag inappropriate or low-quality notes. Once a note reaches the flag threshold, it is hidden automatically and sent to an admin for review.

Fair enforcement

Admins review flagged notes and can approve or remove them. Accounts that break the rules may be warned, suspended, or banned.
